Reading

The control is Item 1 Business, the most boilerplate-heavy item in the corpus, at -4.1011.

Set aside first: Item 3 at 7.7 sentences per section, Item 7A at 18.7 sentences per section. The substantial items run 264, 350, 393 sentences per section. A mean over a handful of sentences per filing describes what the item is made of more than how novel it is. Item 3 in this corpus is largely a cross-reference into the notes, so its position at either end of the table is not evidence about whether legal news is novel.

Against the control, for the substantial items:

- Item 1A Risk Factors (predicted by PLAN): above the control, intervals disjoint. - Item 7 MD&A (predicted by PLAN): above the control, but the intervals overlap.

This is a partial result and the shortfall is the part worth stating. Item 7 MD&A is predicted by PLAN to carry more novelty than boilerplate and does not separate from the control here. 5.2 is a sanity check rather than the test of the measure, and 5.1 passed independently, so this does not invalidate the measure. It does mean the by-item figure cannot be presented as confirming the literature's prediction.
